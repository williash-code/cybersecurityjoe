The 10-Second AI Defense Habit

  • ✓
    Establish a Family Safe Word:

    A secret word never posted online. If a caller sounds like your child or parent in distress, asking for this word halts the scam instantly.

  • ✓
    The Hang-Up & Call-Back Rule:

    Caller ID can be faked. Hang up and dial the saved number directly in your address book.
